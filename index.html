Where the energy goes

Frequently asked questions

What is a kilowatt-hour (kWh)?

A kilowatt-hour is the energy used by a 1,000 watt appliance running for one hour. It is the unit your electricity bill is based on.

Does standby power matter?

Devices on standby draw a small amount continuously. Across a whole home this can add up over a year.

Where does the data on this site come from?

The television charts use the Australian Government Energy Rating registry data exported on 30 September 2026. Other figures are examples only.
